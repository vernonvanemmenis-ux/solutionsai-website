AI Consulting FAQ — Common Questions Answered

Twenty short, honest answers to the questions buyers actually ask before hiring an AI consultant — pricing, qualifications, ROI, timelines, ChatGPT implementation, hiring versus building in-house, red flags in proposals, and what happens after the project ends. Written for mid-market business owners in Johannesburg, across South Africa, and anywhere a video call reaches.

How can AI work in my business?

AI works in your business in three ways: handling repetitive work (admin, ticket triage, document processing), amplifying expert work (drafting, research, analysis), and surfacing patterns you can't see manually (lead scoring, anomaly detection, forecasting). We start by mapping your actual workflows, scoring opportunities by ROI, and shipping the one or two AI systems that move revenue or save the most hours.

What is AI consulting?

AI consulting is strategy and implementation work that turns AI from hype into operational systems. A good AI consultant maps your workflows, identifies the highest-ROI opportunities, designs the system, ships it into production, and trains your team to own it. SolutionsAI does all four — diagnosis through deployment — instead of just writing a slide deck.

How much does AI implementation cost in South Africa?

It depends on scope. An AI Workshop is a few thousand Rand to align your team. An AI Blueprint (the 2–6 week diagnostic with a board-ready business case) typically runs R150K–R400K. Custom AI Projects are scoped per engagement. Long-term Technology Partnerships are retainer-based and tied to outcomes. Book a Strategy Call and we'll size your specific opportunity in 30 minutes.

How long does it take to deploy AI in my business?

An AI Workshop can be delivered in a week. A Blueprint runs 2–6 weeks. A focused Custom AI Project — one workflow, deployed and adopted — typically takes 6–12 weeks from kickoff to production. A full AI Technology Partnership is ongoing with monthly shipping milestones. The biggest predictor of speed is how clean your existing data and processes already are.

What's the difference between AI training and AI consulting?

AI training teaches your team to use existing AI tools well — ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, prompt engineering, internal AI champions. AI consulting designs and builds the AI systems your business runs on — custom agents, automated workflows, integrated tooling. SolutionsAI offers both. Most clients start with training, then graduate into consulting once they see the gap between AI capability and AI use inside their team.

Can AI work for small businesses too?

Yes — though we're most useful to businesses with at least one full-time team doing repeatable work that AI can amplify. If you're a solo operator or have under 5 staff, an AI Workshop or training engagement is usually the right starting point. Custom AI builds make more sense once you have enough volume that the AI saves real hours and pays back the investment.

How do I know if an AI consultant is actually qualified?

Look for three things: real production systems you can verify (not just slide decks or weekend demos), domain expertise that fits your industry, and an honest take on what AI cannot do for you. A qualified AI consultant should walk you through the architecture of past projects, name specific tools and trade-offs, and quote a realistic timeline and budget on the first call. Be wary of anyone selling AI hype before they have understood your workflows. Book a Strategy Call and judge for yourself.

What questions should I ask before hiring an AI consultant?

Five questions cover most of it: (1) Can you show me a system you've shipped that is still in production? (2) How do you decide what NOT to automate? (3) What does the engagement look like after launch — support, iteration, handover? (4) How do you measure success, and what happens if you don't hit it? (5) Who owns the code, the data, and the IP at the end? An AI consultant who flinches at any of those isn't the right fit.

Should I hire an AI consultant or build AI in-house?

Build in-house when you already have a senior AI engineer on staff, the work is core to your competitive moat, and you can wait 6–12+ months for the first production system. Hire an AI consultant when you need to ship faster than you can hire, the work is one cross-functional sprint rather than a permanent team's job, or you want to skip the predictable false starts. Many SolutionsAI clients do both — we ship the first systems and train an internal AI champion to own them long-term.

What does a good AI consultant actually do?

A good AI consultant maps your workflows, identifies the highest-ROI opportunities, designs the system around your people and your stack, ships it into production, and trains your team to own it. A good AI consultant does not write slide decks and disappear, sell generic automations, or pitch AI agents for problems agents won't solve. At SolutionsAI we work through The Compounding Loop — diagnose, build, deploy, measure, learn — and every cycle sharpens the next.

Can AI consultants guarantee specific business results?

No responsible AI consultant guarantees specific business outcomes — too many variables sit outside our control (your data quality, your team's adoption, market dynamics). What we can guarantee is the technical scope of what we build, the timeline we commit to, and that the system passes the success criteria we agree on upfront. For long-term Technology Partnerships we offer revenue-share or performance-aligned commercials so the incentives match.

What's the difference between AI strategy and AI implementation consulting?

AI strategy is the work of deciding what to build and why — mapping workflows, scoring opportunities, sizing the business case. AI implementation is the work of actually shipping the systems — designing, building, integrating, training. Most consulting firms only do one or the other; SolutionsAI does both, because strategy without implementation is a slide deck and implementation without strategy is a shiny tool nobody uses. Our AI Blueprint is the strategy deliverable; the AI Project is the implementation.

Should I hire a boutique AI specialist or a large consulting firm?

Large consulting firms give you brand-name credibility, multi-workstream scale, and standardised processes — at higher cost and with less senior attention on your account. Boutique AI specialists like SolutionsAI give you the actual people who'll do the work, faster iteration, no junior consultants billed at senior rates, and direct accountability. Pick large if you're a regulated enterprise that needs a procurement-friendly vendor; pick boutique if you want the work done well and fast.

How do I measure ROI from AI consulting services?

Measure it like any business investment: cost in (project fee + your internal time) versus value out (hours saved × loaded hourly cost, revenue moved, error rate reduced, ticket volume handled). For each project we agree on a small set of measurable success criteria upfront, instrument the system to track them, and review them at hand-over and again 90 days post-launch. The cheapest mistake is shipping without metrics; the next-cheapest is measuring vanity metrics. Book a Strategy Call and we'll pick the right ones for your case.

What happens after the AI consulting project ends?

Three options depending on what we agreed: (1) Handover — we train your team to own the system, ship the documentation, and step away. (2) Retainer — we stay on as an AI Technology Partner for ongoing iteration, monitoring, and roadmap. (3) Phased — we hand over the core system and stay on for new builds. Whichever option, the code, the data, and the IP belong to you. We don't build systems that hold clients hostage.

What red flags should I watch for in AI consulting proposals?

Watch for vague deliverables ("we'll build an AI solution"), pricing without a defined scope, no measurable success criteria, no plan for handover or maintenance, lock-in clauses on data or IP, and promises that AI will replace specific job roles wholesale on an aggressive timeline. A clean proposal names the workflow, the system, the timeline, the price, the success criteria, and the ownership terms. If any of those are missing — ask.

What's the difference between AI consulting and software development?

Software development is the work of building software given a spec — write the code that does the thing. AI consulting is the work of figuring out what AI software to build and why — which workflow, which model, what success looks like, what trade-offs are acceptable — and then either building it or coordinating the build. Hire a software developer when you know the feature to build; hire an AI consultant when you don't yet know what to build, or whether AI is even the right tool.
